The problem

Parents shouldn't have to
watch every second of the day.

For children with autism or ADHD, emotional escalations follow a pattern — visible in the body long before they become visible in behavior. Yet most parents feel they have no choice but to stay permanently alert.

This constant vigilance comes at a profound cost. Many parents reduce their working hours or leave the workforce entirely — not by choice, but because staying alert feels like the only way to protect their child.

⚡

Escalations have early signs

Heart rate and HRV change measurably in the body minutes before a meltdown becomes visible in behavior.

👁

Parents carry the full load

Constant observation is exhausting and unsustainable. Parents deserve a reliable signal — not another task.

⏱

Timing is everything

Intervening early — before the peak — makes all the difference. Too late, and it's a crisis. Early enough, and it's a calm adjustment.

💙

Children deserve calm, not tension

When parents are constantly watching and anxious, children feel it. A timely signal creates space for calm, not crisis.

The solution

A wearable and app that reads what the body already knows.

1

The wearable monitors in real time

Heart rate and HRV are tracked continuously — no effort required from the child or parent.

2

Ques detects early patterns

When biosignals indicate a rising escalation, Ques recognises the pattern — long before it becomes visible.

3

Parents receive a timely alert

A gentle notification gives parents the window they need to adjust the environment, reduce the trigger, or offer support.
